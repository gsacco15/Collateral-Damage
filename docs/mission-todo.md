   (people.ts already has "the night caretaker" there at night). You can't strike a school, so you draw him out.
   The baker's new line says so: `voice/mission-baker-school.mp3` (recorded).
2. **Strike specific places first.** Two of the Engineer's places, each with its own props once the file reaches them:
   - a lock-up in the Workshops, full of stencilled crates, drums and a covered pickup (day; stored weapons go off);
   - a smuggling boat under the camp footbridge, loaded with crates, a guard with a lantern (night: the night mission).
   Burn both and Samir rides out of the school. Handler line: `voice/mission-drawout.mp3` (recorded).
3. **Hidden final weapon, one shot.** "The Needle": no explosive, weight and blades (tiny blast, very tight landing).
   Hidden in the weapon list until the last clue. The aim starts a few metres off him: you have to drag it onto him.
   One shot; a miss and he's gone. Handler line: `voice/mission-needle.mp3` (recorded).
   Still to record: a drone hum, and the Needle's fall and hit.
